## 2. What changed on Nov 11 (all live from the Nov 12 card)
- **Opponent-current early method** (`jobs/ratings_core.py`, `OPP_CURRENT = True`, commit 54e9778): for teams with <3 D-I games, each game is judged against the opponent's current rating (leave-one-out) instead of its preseason prior. 198 teams moved on Nov 11, 3 by 0.5+ (max 0.63). Jon's decision.
- **Scoreboard check job** (`jobs/scoreboard.py`; runs in the Daily card job after ratings and again in Grade with the new closes) → `data/ratings/scoreboard_<date>.csv`, one row per team: raw vs close, 3P luck, FT luck (own, opp), adjusted vs close, λ × adjusted per game (`scoreboard_move`), `composite_move`, `gap`, FT talent. **FT luck:** each side's FTM minus its FT talent × FTA. Talent = player-based projection (prior-season FT% shrunk with 40 pseudo-attempts, FTA-weighted; new players 0.706; +0.9 pt) blended with observed at FTA/(FTA+220). Opponent FT% is pure noise to a defense (split-half ≈0); own FT% is ~0.3–0.4 reliable at 5 games, ~0.5 at 10–13. The composite is unchanged (it uses FT *rate*). First use corrected a read: SDSU's opponents missed ~6 points of FTs, so SDSU ran ~3.8/game worse than the closes, not even — the composite's −0.9 was right.
