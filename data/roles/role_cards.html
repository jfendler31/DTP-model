Role cardsCompetitive possessions only · through Nov. 12
Every second of floor time is weighted by how live the game was: full weight while the leading team's win probability is 95% or lower, none at 99.9%+, scaled in between. Min% is the share of the team's competitive minutes he was on the floor. Usg is the share of team possessions he used while on the floor. Ast% (assist rate) is the share of teammates' makes he assisted while on the floor, which depends on who he plays with; Ast ratio is assists per 100 plays he finished (shots, free-throw trips, assists, turnovers), which doesn't. Plays splits those plays into shoot, assist and turnover; tap or hover the bar for the numbers. Self is the share of his made shots nobody assisted. FTr is free-throw attempts per 100 field-goal attempts. Shots are raw makes-attempts, all minutes. Shot mix splits his attempts into rim, midrange and three; tap or hover for the shares and how many of his rim and three makes were assisted. More columns adds eFG, turnover rate, offensive rebound rate, steals and blocks per 40. Players under 20 competitive minutes or 6 shots are marked limited sample. A dashed tag rests on a thin sample: under 10 made shots for lead engine and self-creating scorer, 6 rim makes for the rim tags, 6 made threes for the shooter tags, 20 shots for the shot-type tags, 25 for foul drawer, 60 competitive minutes for the passing tags and 80 for glass, putbacks and the defensive tags. Hover a dashed tag for its count.
